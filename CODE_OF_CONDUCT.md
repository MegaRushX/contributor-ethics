# Code of Conduct

This applies to this repository: the charter, its record, and discussions about it.

## What we expect from anyone here

- Respect the people you are talking to. They have lives outside this repository.
- Argue about the work, not about the person.
- Give the reason for a claim, and give the source for a factual claim.
- Say what you tested and what you did not.
- Accept that a maintainer's decision on their own project is theirs.
- Criticism of a contribution is not hostility toward the contributor.
- No harassment, no gatekeeping by status, no bad-faith arguments, no dogpiling.

## What we commit to in our own contributions

The operating rules in [RULES.md](RULES.md) are our conduct rules for outside projects. Project rules in a project we contribute to outrank them.
